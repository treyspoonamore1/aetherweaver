# Phase 4e — Bonds

*2026-10-30 · plan: [docs/plan/02-game-design.md §6](plan/02-game-design.md#6-heroes-and-ai), [03 §5](plan/03-content-bible.md#5-heroes) · follows [Phase 4d](phase-4d.md)*

The last piece of the Loom: **Bonds**, the heroes growing with you. Kael, Lyra and Vexis each earn **Bond XP** from the fights you share, most of all from what you do for them. Their Bond **ranks** (1–10) open **talents** that reshape their quirks and kits, an **alternate Solo**, and two short **scenes** each, which give them stories. This is the plan's "heroes are characters, not stat sticks" (pillar 4). The user chose how it works:

| Question | Choice |
|---|---|
| How much now? | **Everything** for the three starters: ranks 1–10, a talent pick (1 of 2) at ranks 3, 6 and 9 (18 talents), an alternate Solo at rank 10, and two scenes each. |
| How is Bond XP earned? | **A base plus your support.** Every fight pays each hero in it some Bond XP, and more for their share of your support: healing, shields, amplified damage and saves. The hero you carry grows fastest, and lost fights still pay. |
| Can talents change? | **Freely, between runs**, like the Astrolabe. A run keeps the talents it embarked with. |
| Where do scenes play? | **At Respites.** When a hero has a scene waiting, a Respite offers it as a fourth choice instead of resting: no healing, but Bond XP for that hero and a short story beat. |

## How it works

- **Bond XP.** After every fight, each hero in the party earns:
  - a **base**, the same for all three;
  - a share of a **support pool**, in proportion to your support of them that fight: your healing and shields on them, the damage your songs and Thread added to theirs, and the life your saves kept.

  Both scale with the fight: an elite pays more, a mini-boss more again, the boss most. A lost fight pays half. The Summary shows what each hero earned, and the Result shows the fight's.
- **Ranks 1–10.** Each rank costs more than the last (see *Bond XP and ranks*). A rank-up gets a card, like a Weaver Rank's.
- **Talents.** At Bond ranks **3, 6 and 9** a hero opens a tier of two talents: choose one, or neither. Talents change *how* a hero fights: they reshape a quirk, add a move, or tie the hero to your Thread, your field or your Focus. Numbers come second (pillar 5).
- **The alternate Solo.** At rank **10** a hero opens a second Solo. Choose which one they bring. The defaults deal damage or shield; the alternates are new ways to support the party, each with its own decision for you.
- **Changing them.** Choose and change talents and Solos freely between runs: on the Bonds screen, from the Title or from Prep. Embarking freezes them for the run, as it does the Astrolabe and the carried relics. The Training Grounds play with your current choices.
- **Scenes.** Each hero has two: one at Bond rank 2 and one at rank 7. Once a hero reaches a scene's rank, it **waits** until a Respite offers it. Choosing it gives up the Respite's rest, meditation or retune for:
  - the scene: about 20 seconds of dialogue, with one reply to choose (it changes their answer, not the game);
  - Bond XP for that hero, worth a good share of a rank.

  A scene plays once. Scenes you've seen can be read again on the Bonds screen.
- **Opening.** Bonds need no Weaver Rank: Bond XP is earned from the first fight. The Bonds screen opens with the first Bond rank-up, which introduces it.
- **Skip the unlocks** lets every talent and both Solos be chosen, as if every Bond were at rank 10. Scenes still wait for real ranks.

## The talents

Each tier's two talents are a real choice: different ways to play, not a stronger and a weaker. The numbers are tuned against the harness (see *Harness*).

### Kael, the Bulwark

Kael needs to be needed. His talents turn Glory Hound from a liability into a tool, and his shield from his own into the party's.

| Rank | Talent | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Steady Vanguard** | Glory Hound waits for your Focus: when a new pack arrives, Kael charges only if you've set a Focus, and charges that enemy. |
| 3 | **Vanguard's Roar** | When Glory Hound sends him charging, everything within 8 m of his quarry turns on him for 4 s. |
| 6 | **Riposte Bash** | When a Ward on Kael takes a blow, he answers with Shield Bash at the attacker, if it's within 6 m, whatever its cooldown (once every 4 s). A Perfect Ward does every time, and sends back half the blow. |
| 6 | **Bulwark of Faith** | While you Thread him, Kael is healed 20% more. |
| 9 | **Unbreakable** | Once a fight, a blow that would down Kael leaves him at 1 life, untouchable for 1 s. |
| 9 | **Shieldbearer** | Uncued, Aegis Wall also shields every hero within 10 m of Kael with 75% of his barrier. He raises it himself when one of them falls below 30% of their life. |

### Lyra, the Windrunner

Lyra trusts no one to have her back. Her talents either keep her alive on her own, or bind her to your Thread and field.

| Rank | Talent | Effect |
|---|---|---|

### Vexis, the Starcaller

Vexis thinks the song can be solved like an equation. Her talents make her commitment pay, or carry her spells along your Thread and into your field.

| Rank | Talent | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Arcane Ward** | When an enemy strikes Vexis up close, she blinks 6 m away from it (once every 12 s). |
| 3 | **Overchannel** | Starbolt deals 40% more damage and takes 10% longer to cast. |
| 6 | **Starthread** | Starbolt also strikes the enemy nearest the hero you Thread (if it isn't Vexis), at 20% damage. |
| 6 | **Event Horizon** | Gravity Well reaches 2 m farther, and pulls the enemies it catches into your field: toward the point within three quarters of its radius nearest the well, never onto you. |
| 9 | **Supernova** | Starfall ends in a nova: every enemy within 6 m of its last meteor takes five meteors' damage. |
| 9 | **Constellation** | Each Cue leaves a star where the hero stands, casting Vexis's arcane bolt at the nearest enemy every 1.5 s for 6 s. |

## Bond XP and ranks

First guesses, to be tuned against the harness (see *Harness*). The aim: a hero's first talent in their first run or two, the second around run 6, the third around run 14, and the alternate Solo around run 18. The hero you support most should be a few runs ahead.

- **Per fight**, for each hero in the party: `kind × (base + pool × share)`.
  - `base` 12 and `pool` 30 (shared among the three by your support). The first guess, 8 and 24, was too slow: see *Harness*.
  - `kind`: a skirmish 1, an elite 1.5, a mini-boss 2, the boss 3. A lost fight pays half.
  - `share`: the hero's part of your support that fight. Without any support, the pool isn't paid.
- **Your support of a hero** (`supportReceived`, plus the damage you amplified):
  - your healing of them: auras, Mend and the rest, but not their own, and not the extra that Bulwark of Faith gives Kael;
  - the damage your shields took for them, the Martyr's Veil included, and what a cued Celestial Sphere swallowed (after armour);
  - the life a Perfect Ward, a Rewind or a Reel kept out of a hit's reach, up to the life they had;
  - the life a Mend revive gives back, and the life a Chime keeps by cleansing a lethal Dirge;
  - the damage your songs and Thread added to theirs. Their own talents' marks (Hunter's Mark, Hawk's Vigil) are theirs.
- **A scene** pays 40% of the XP between its rank and the next.
- **Ranks**, cumulative XP: 0, 80, 220, 420, 680, 1000, 1380, 1820, 2320, 2900.

## Power

Talents and Solos add power, but the plan caps meta *raw* power (the Astrolabe and the Reliquary, about +30%), and Bonds are meant to change how heroes play more than how hard they hit. The harness measures each talent as the enemy life it's worth, as for Dissonance. The targets:

- a tier's two talents within a few percent of each other;
- a hero's full Bond (three talents and either Solo) worth about 5–8% of enemy life;
- all three heroes' full Bonds about 15–20%.

## Contracts (v13)

**Content** (`CONTENT_VERSION` bump), in `src/content/bonds.ts`:
- `bondRules`: the ranks' cumulative XP, `base`, `pool`, the per-kind multipliers, the loss multiplier, the talent ranks (3, 6, 9), the Solo rank (10), the scenes' XP share.
- `talents`: `{ id, hero, tier (1–3), name, text, values }`, two per tier per hero. The text is built from `values`.
- `altSolos`: `{ id, hero, name, text, cuedText, chargeTime, duration, values }`.
- `scenes`: `{ id, hero, rank, title, lines: { speaker, text }[], replies: { text, response }[] }`.
- Checks: every hero with Bonds has two talents a tier, one alternate Solo and two scenes, and texts fill from values (the content schema). Every talent and Solo has its hook in the sim (a test against `KNOWN_TALENTS` and `KNOWN_ALT_SOLOS` in `src/sim/bonds.ts`).

**Sim** (`SIM_VERSION` 18; 19 after the review's fixes and the tuning):
- `SimConfig.bonds?: { talents: string[]; solos: string[] }`: the talents in force and the alternate Solos chosen. Resolution: unknown ids refused, a hero not in the party ignored, one talent per tier per hero. An absent Weaver's re-run keeps them: the heroes are the same people.
- `HeroComponent`:
  - `talents` and `soloId` (the Solo this hero brings), for the views;
  - `supportReceived`: your healing, shields and saves on them this fight (Bond XP);
  - each talent's state where it needs one (a cooldown, once a fight).
- Each talent's effect at its hook: Glory Hound, the Perfect Ward, healing received, the down check, Aegis Wall, telegraph dodging, attack range and speed, landing hits, Focus damage, Gale Barrage, Starbolt, Gravity Well, Starfall, Cues.
- The alternate Solos in `solos.ts`, alongside the defaults.
- Event `talentFired { hero, talent, x, z, target? }` whenever a talent visibly acts, for the views and sounds.

**Meta** (`src/meta/bonds.ts`, pure):
- `bondRank(xp)`, `bondProgress(xp)`, `tierRank(tier)`, `soloRank()`, `rankOpens(hero, rank)`;
- `bondXpForFight(outcome, kind)`: Bond XP by hero; `addBondXp(bonds, xpByHero)`, with the ranks reached;
- `talentChoices(hero, tier)`, `altSoloOf(hero)`, `fitBond(hero, bond, rank?)`;
- `runBonds(bonds, heroes, all)`: what a run's heroes play with, `RunBond { heroId, talents, solo }`; `fitRunBonds(bonds)` for a saved run;
- `bondsConfig(runBonds)`: the run's frozen choices as `SimConfig.bonds`;
- `sceneWaiting(hero, bond)` and `sceneXp(scene)`.

**Profile v6:**
- `bonds: Record<heroId, { xp, talents: (id | null)[3], solo: 'default' | 'alt', seen: Record<sceneId, reply> }>`.
- Fitted on load: unknown ids and a talent in the wrong tier dropped. A choice above the hero's rank is kept, for Skip the unlocks: a run plays only what each hero's rank opens (`runBonds`).

**Run:**
- `RunState.bonds: RunBond[]`: the choices, followed in Prep (`setBonds`) and frozen at embark. Old saves have none; a saved run keeps only the talents and Solos the content knows.
- `EncounterResult.bondXp: Record<heroId, number>`.
- A Respite's fourth choice: `bondScene(run, sceneId)` goes to a `scene` phase with `RunState.scene { heroId, sceneId }`; `endScene(run)` returns to the map and records the scene in `RunState.scenes`, so the Summary counts its Bond XP. A saved run whose scene is gone goes back to its Respite.

**Stores:**
- The profile store: `awardBonds(xpByHero)` (with `bondNotices` for the rank-up cards), `setTalent(hero, tier, id | null)`, `setSolo(hero, 'default' | 'alt')`, `seeScene(id, reply)` (records the reply and pays the scene's Bond XP), `bondsAll()` (Skip the unlocks) and `runBonds(heroes)`.
- The run store: Prep follows the profile's choices, and so does a run resumed in Prep; a fight's Bond XP is awarded with its Weaver XP. `scenesWaiting()`, `bondScene(id)` and `endScene(reply)` play a Respite's scene.

## Presentation

**Screens** ([docs/phase-4e/ui.md](phase-4e/ui.md), with screenshots of every view):

- **The Bonds screen** opens from the Title and from Prep, with a tab per hero. Each page shows:
  - the Bond rank and XP bar, and the ranks 1–10 as a track marked where each opens something;
  - the three talent tiers, two toggles each (choose one, or neither);
  - their own Solo and the alternate, side by side, with both lines;
  - their scenes: seen ones can be read again, waiting ones say so.

  Locked tiers name their rank. Skip the unlocks opens everything but the scenes.
- **The Title** has a Bonds button with each hero's rank. **Prep** has a Bonds row and names each hero's Solo and talents for the run.
- **The Result** shows each fight's Bond XP per hero, with a bar. **The Summary** shows the run's: its fights' and its scenes'.
- **A Bond rank-up** gets a card saying what the rank opened; the first one introduces Bonds.
- **A Respite** offers a waiting scene ("Sit with Kael", key 4). **The scene** has the hero by the fire, the lines, two replies (1 and 2), their answer and the Bond XP it pays.
- **In a fight**, each frame shows a Bond rank badge. Its tooltip names the Solo the hero brought and the talents in force. Unbreakable and Supernova get a callout when they act.
- **The Codex** has a Bonds page.

## Harness

`tools/bonds.ts` measures Bonds; `tools/harness-core.ts`'s `playRun` takes a run's `bonds`.

- `--pace`: Bond XP a run, and the runs each hero needs to reach each tier.
- `--find`: the build's win rate at each Threat.
- By default: each talent's and Solo's worth, as the enemy life it's worth. `--sets` does the same for each hero's whole Bond, and everyone's.
- `--fires`: how often each talent acts in a fight.
- `--bot skilled` uses the Skilled bot. `--tree` uses the Rank-20 build (a full Astrolabe and a typical carried set). `--sets --hero kael` measures one hero's Bonds alone, so heroes can run side by side.

## Code review

An independent review of the core (content, sim, meta and stores) confirmed that, without Bonds, the golden scenarios and full bot runs are unchanged, that replays round-trip the choices, and that Bond XP, the choices' fitting and freezing, scenes and old saves behave as designed. What it found, all fixed with tests:

- **High: a saved run with an unknown talent, Solo or scene crashed the game.** A talent renamed by tuning made every fight of a resumed run throw. `loadRun` now keeps only what the content knows (`fitRunBonds`), and a run saved mid-scene whose scene is gone goes back to its Respite.
- **Fermata's hold got around Unbreakable:** a hold that ran out on Kael downed him with Unbreakable unspent. Both paths now share one check.
- **Supernova used a stale strike point** when a Starfall dropped no meteor: it now bursts only after one struck.
- **Oathkeeper with Kael untouchable made the heroes beside him immune:** hits landed on him and vanished. While he's untouchable or held, hits land where they were aimed. A redirected hit's status (a Dirge's silence) now goes to Kael. By design, a hit he takes skips the target's own shields: it lands on him instead.
- **Credit for Bond XP:**
  - Reel and Chime saves, Rewind spares and Mend revives paid no support; now they do.
  - Bulwark of Faith's extra healing was credited to you; it's Kael's.
  - A cued Celestial Sphere and a Perfect Ward credited the raw blow; now they credit the life they kept.
- **Ricochet bounced inside a suppressed field**, with the Weaver down; and with Hawk Eye, the bounce rang Resonance a second time.
- **Smaller:**
  - Wind Step fired while Lyra was stunned, and a step ending inside a rooted Gale Barrage let her start a cast.
  - Constellation's stars kept casting after Vexis fell.
  - A non-finite Bond XP value could reach the save.
  - Resuming a run in Prep kept its old Bonds.
  - Hero calls ignored Overchannel's longer cast.
  - Shieldbearer scaled with each ally's life rather than Kael's barrier.
- **UI:**
  - The Training Grounds now play with your choices.
  - The HUD names the Solo a hero brought.
- **The doc:**
  - It now says that Storm Queen and Starthread need another hero threaded, and that Shieldbearer is uncued.
  - It now matches the code on `seen`, scenes and the store names.
  - It notes that loading keeps choices above a hero's rank, so toggling Skip the unlocks loses nothing.
  - A test checks that every talent and Solo in the content has its hook in the sim.

## Known gaps

- **Only the three starting heroes have Bonds.** A hero added later needs six talents, an alternate Solo and two scenes; the content checks catch a hero that has some but not all.
- **The bots don't play every talent as a player would.** The Novice never sets a Focus, so Steady Vanguard and Hunter's Mark do nothing for it, and it rarely Perfect-Wards (Riposte Bash). Those are judged by the Skilled bot (see *Harness*), which Focuses only casters it wants interrupted.
- **Solos are rare in short fights.** A Solo charges in 25 s, so it plays in about a third of the Novice's fights; that caps what the alternate Solos and Supernova can be worth.
- **Scenes are short and fixed:** two a hero, one reply each, and the reply changes the hero's answer, not the game. Scenes wait for a Respite, so a run that skips Respites skips them.
- **No Bond XP in the Training Grounds.** They play with your choices, but a sandbox fight pays nothing.
