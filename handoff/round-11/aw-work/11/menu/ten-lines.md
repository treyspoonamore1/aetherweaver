# Ten lines, before and after

The ten that improved most, as a player reads them. Placeholders are filled with what a first run
shows.

**1. The Respite's opening line**

- Before: The choir cannot reach this alcove. Take one: tend to your wounds, sit with the song, or retune your songs.
- After: Choose one.

The three choices are the three cards right under it.

**2. Start fresh, on the demo's first screen**

- Before: A new Weaver: the real first hours. Every station of the Loom stays dark until it opens, and shows you around when it does.
- After: The game from the beginning. Things unlock as you play.

**3. The Loom's welcome, first card**

- Before: Between runs you come back here. Each station is a place to go: to set out, to practise, to read up and, as you play, to grow the Weaver. The dark ones open one by one, and the first visit to each shows you around.
- After: You come back here after every run. Lit stations are open. Dark ones open as you play, and each shows you around on your first visit.

**4. A closed station in the Loom**

- Before: A star map of bonuses. Opens at Weaver Rank 4.
- After: Opens at Rank 4

"A star map of bonuses" shows when you point at the station or move to it with the keys, and a
screen reader still reads both sentences.

**5. A locked Calling in Prep**

- Before: Reach Weaver Rank 7 to choose it (or Settings → Skip the unlocks).
- After: nothing. The lock on the card already says "Rank 7".

The same card also lost its two sentences of rules. They are behind its "See the path" button.

**6. Choosing a party of three**

- Before: Choose three. They take the keys 1, 2 and 3 in this order, so a hero's key can change with the party: the key each will take is on their card.
- After: Choose three. Each card shows the key its hero will take: 1, 2 or 3.

**7. The Merchant**

- Before: A pack-mule of a merchant, all bells and pockets. Pay in Aether; what you buy is yours for the rest of the run.
- After: Pay in Aether. Each item sells once and lasts the run.

"All bells and pockets" moved to the small line above the title, where it replaced "A merchant ·
each item once".

**8. A coach hint**

- Before: Everyone is healthy — press RMB to switch the Thread to Empower and add damage
- After: Everyone is healthy. Press RMB for Empower and add damage

Sixty-eight hints lost their dash this way. The fifteen that still have one are about auras,
Solos, Cues, Resonance or a Crescendo, which this pass may not touch.

**9. Calls, in How to Play**

- Before: Before a big move a hero calls for help: a gold glyph on their frame shows what they want, its key and a countdown. Answer in time and the move becomes a Flourish.
- After: A gold glyph on a frame is a hero's call, with its key and a countdown. Answer in time for a Flourish.

**10. Retuning at the Forge**

- Before: The forge's fire takes your old songs and gives them back reshaped. It costs 20 Aether and is this forge's one service: nothing else here can be used after.
- After: Retuning costs 20 Aether and uses up this forge.
